Metals

Metals are generally lustrous malleable ductile, and good conductors of heat and electricity. Metals are also hard and have high melting points. They are sonorous, meaning they produce a ringing sound when struck. Most metals are solid at room temperature, except mercury. They tend to form positive ions by losing electrons during chemical reactions, and hence are reducing agents in chemical reactions.

Non-metals

Non-metals are a group of elements that typically do not conduct heat or electricity and are generally not lustrous(except iodine). They are often brittle and cannot be malleable or ductile. Unlike metals, non-metals tend to gain electrons during chemical reactions and become electroneagtive.
